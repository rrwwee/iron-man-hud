# Globe outlines

Made with Natural Earth. The data is public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Source (fetched 2026-10-12):
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

Derivative: `globe-land.json`, exterior rings with at least eight source vertices, every second vertex retained, coordinates rounded to two decimal places. 102 rings / 2,592 points / 38,652 bytes. Used as coarse land outlines on a manually positioned globe, not as precise boundaries or live location data.
