# Iron Man HUD

A cinematic personal website whose visitors explore content through an interactive helmet HUD.

## Language

**Face**:
The on-screen depiction of Rory, reconstructed from his own reference footage, whose gaze and head movement respond to the visitor.

**HUD**:
The helmet's visible interface surrounding the face, through which visitors discover and open content.
_Avoid_: Hood

**Panel**:
A content window opened within the HUD, keeping the visitor inside the experience.

**Menu item**:
A selectable entry in the HUD that opens a Panel, plays a small audio item, or leads to an external site.

**Demo**:
The first desktop experience intended to be shared publicly for its humor or visual appeal.

**Section**:
One content destination selected through the HUD: suit/activity, flight history, video/audio, CV, About, or personal links.

**Preview panel**:
The small content window adjoining the circular menu, displaying the selected section.

**Expanded panel**:
The larger right-hand HUD window occupied by a section moved out of the preview choices.

**Centre**:
The circular menu's interior, containing the three-chamber instrument hub.

**Instrument**:
A HUD graphic whose state represents an actual measurement or supports an action, such as a cursor dial or public-activity graph.

**Flight history**:
The owner's completed flights, shown as routes on the globe. Upcoming and cancelled flights are excluded.
