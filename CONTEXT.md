# Iron Man HUD

A cinematic, interactive web experience intended for visitors to explore and share.

## Language

**Face**:
The on-screen depiction of Tony Stark inside the helmet, whose gaze and head movement respond to the visitor.

**HUD**:
The helmet's visible interface surrounding the face, through which visitors discover and open content.
_Avoid_: Hood

**Panel**:
A content window opened within the HUD, keeping the visitor inside the experience.

**Demo**:
The first desktop experience intended to be shared publicly for its humor or visual appeal.
