# Media permission routes

Research checked 15 October 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). This records primary-source evidence and practical clearance routes; it is not a legal opinion or proof that permission has been granted. No rights holder was contacted.

## Joseph: the Donny Osmond film

Universal's current [official film page](https://www.universalpicturesathome.com/movies/joseph-and-the-amazing-technicolor-dreamcoat) identifies the Donny Osmond production, offers digital purchase/rental, and carries a Universal copyright notice. The page labels its release 2000; this is the film referred to in this project as the 1999 film. Its listing is evidence of Universal's involvement, not a complete chain of title or a grant of website rights.

Start with Universal's [Media Licensing portal](https://www.universalclips.com/). Its older contact/departments URL now redirects to Universal Destinations & Experiences, so it is not a verified film-rights contact. The latter explicitly handles clips, stills and elements; do **not** assume it offers full-film streaming rights. Ask it to identify or route the full-feature digital exhibition/streaming rights team for the relevant territory, and confirm whether Universal can authorize all required uses. The [official film page](https://www.universalpicturesathome.com/movies/joseph-and-the-amazing-technicolor-dreamcoat) is a suitable authorized destination if streaming clearance is unavailable.

An amateur stage licence is not the answer. [ALW Show Licensing's sample Joseph agreement](https://alwshowlicensing.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/Joseph-Adult-Amateur-Sugar-licence-2024.pdf) grants stage rights and expressly reserves internet, recording and other rights. Its [FAQ](https://alwshowlicensing.com/faq/) discusses permission to film a licensee's *own production* in the UK and Eire, not redistribution of the existing Donny Osmond film. ALW/RUG can be asked to route rights questions, but a school or amateur performance licence would not clear this use.

For the current Internet Archive stream, there is no clearance established by these sources. Recommended publication decision: disable that full-film stream pending explicit permission; point to an authorized viewing service instead. Do not treat an uploader's availability or technical embed capability as evidence of authorization.

## Iron Man dialogue, still and wider character use

Disney's [official licensing FAQ](https://www.disneystudiolicensing.com/) specifically says Marvel Studios manages rights to its films, starting with *Iron Man* (2008), and directs written requests to **permissions@marvel.com**. It uses the same address for commercial and noncommercial Marvel IP requests and warns that responses may take weeks. This is a better initial route than sending this request to an unrelated Disney division.

Send the actual proposed uses together: the short movie-dialogue audio, the exact Tony Stark still, the Face rendering, the recognizable suit/character imagery, and branding references. Ask which ones Marvel can authorize and what separate approvals remain. Do not imply that licensing the audio automatically licenses the still, character imagery or actor likeness.

Disney's [clip/still request form](https://www.disneystudiolicensing.com/licensing-request-form/) illustrates clearance information: property/title, exact asset description, context, distribution medium, territory, term and dates. Its disclaimer notes that submitting a request is not permission and that required third-party consents/releases remain the licensee's responsibility. The [licensing FAQ](https://www.disneystudiolicensing.com/) describes negotiated licences for specified media, territory and term. There is no published blanket free-use exception for personal portfolios in this material.

Recommended publication decision: replace/disable the copied dialogue and still until cleared or until a qualified adviser has reviewed a specific applicable exception. Their brevity alone is insufficient. The wider Face and recognizable character/suit assets deserve their own source-and-licence inventory; a media-only review does not clear the rest of the site.

## Carts of Darkness / NFB

NFB provides a conditional embedding licence, not blanket redistribution permission. See its [terms](https://help.nfb.ca/terms/) and the [final audit](media-rights-audit-2026-10-15.md) for the use restrictions. For confirmation, use [NFB's contact form](https://help.nfb.ca/contact-the-nfb/) or its [digital-platform sales contact](https://www.nfb.ca/distribution/sales-team), Grace Marzella, g.marzella@nfb.ca. ## Information to include in a clearance request

Prepare one concise request with:

- Your legal name/entity, country, contact and exact website/domain.
- A screenshot or private demo link showing the HUD context and any transformations, player masks or overlapping graphics.
- Exact production, asset/frame, source and timecodes; full film versus excerpt; clip duration; audio-only versus audiovisual; whether downloading or hosting occurs.
- Public on-demand website use, desktop/mobile, autoplay/sound behavior, and whether the player is official or custom.
- Audience and purpose: personal professional portfolio, promotion of services/employment, and whether ads, fees, sponsorship or revenue are involved.
- Requested countries (worldwide if accessible worldwide), term, launch date, expected reach, permitted edits and attribution.
- A request for written confirmation of the rights covered, fees, required credits, restrictions, and any music, performer/likeness, guild or other clearances excluded.

These are practical scoping details, not a promise that a rights holder will license the use. Do not launch on the strength of an unanswered email.

## Copyright exceptions: jurisdiction matters

The [U.S. Copyright Office's fair-use guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html) applies four factors to the actual facts: purpose, nature, amount/substantiality and market effect. Noncommercial use is not automatically fair, nor does a short excerpt automatically qualify. Its [FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-fairuse.html) rejects fixed word/note/percentage safe harbours and recommends permission when in doubt. A movie excerpt used as decoration or entertainment differs from a quotation needed for criticism or analysis; that is a risk assessment, not a court ruling about this site.

In the UK, the [IPO's official exceptions guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/exceptions-to-copyright) describes particular purposes, including criticism/review/quotation and parody/caricature/pastiche, subject to fair dealing and, where required, acknowledgement. It is not a general “personal website” exemption. Necessity, amount and market substitution matter.

For Ireland, start with [IPOI's legislation index](https://www.ipoi.gov.ie/en/law-practice/legislation/copyright/copyright-acts/) and current amended law, rather than importing U.S. fair use. IPOI's [consolidated Copyright and Related Rights Act](https://www.ipoi.gov.ie/en/law-practice/legislation/copyright/copyright-acts/consolidated-copyright-act-2000.pdf) includes purpose-specific fair-dealing provisions; section 50 defines fairness with reference to lawful public availability, purpose, extent and unreasonable prejudice. The consolidation may not include the latest amendments: verify current legislation before reliance. A public portfolio does not become private study merely because it is personally owned.
